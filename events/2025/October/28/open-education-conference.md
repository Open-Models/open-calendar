## Open Education Conference

- Begin: 28/10/2025
- End: 30/10/2025
- Location: Denver & Online
- Categories: open education

Event information : https://openeducationconference.org/

The Open Education Conference (“OpenEd”) is an annual convening for sharing and learning about open educational
resources, open pedagogy, and open education initiatives. This engaging event brings together over 1,600 educators and
open education advocates in-person and online to collaborate, support, and drive strategies for education that are
accessible, affordable, and reflective of the learners they serve. 

The Open Education Conference community has gathered annually since 2004—originally in person, then online, and now as
an inclusive hybrid event. Deeply rooted in the community, the conference is led by a community-elected [board of
directors](https://openeducationconference.org/about/board) and driven by a shared [strategic
vision](https://openeducationconference.org/about/strategic-vision). Conference operations are currently housed within
SPARC.
